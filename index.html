Research & Projects

HER‑RAG: Hybrid‑Evidence Retrieval Enhanced RAG for Hallucination Mitigation

This work proposes an end‑to‑end RAG framework that suppresses LLM hallucinations collaboratively from both retrieval‑side and generation‑side perspectives. The architecture integrates two‑layer sparse‑dense hybrid evidence retrieval, attention‑driven dynamic evidence weighting‑and‑filtering, together with a factual‑consistency auxiliary joint loss function. Evaluated on TruthfulQA and HotpotQA benchmarks, the system achieves factual‑consistency F1‑score of 0.846 and hallucination‑detection AUC of 0.881. Compared against vanilla Llama‑3‑8B baseline, hallucination ratio decreases by 22.7%; compared with conventional single‑dense‑retrieval RAG, hallucination ratio drops by 13.5%.

Keywords: Generative‑AI; Large Language Model; Retrieval‑Augmented Generation; Hallucination Mitigation; Factual Consistency; Hybrid Sparse‑Dense Retrieval

Publications

Hybrid‑Evidence Retrieval RAG Model for Hallucination Mitigation in AIGC Text Generation
Hu, Zhixin. Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology, University of Malaya, Malaysia.

Abstract: Generative Artificial Intelligence large language models deliver powerful free‑form text generation capabilities for intelligent writing, open‑domain question‑answering, and automated content creation. Nevertheless, the inherent hallucination phenomenon severely undermines factual reliability of generated outputs, where LLMs produce linguistically fluent but factually fabricated statements, fake citations, and erroneous causal deductions. This paper proposes HER‑RAG, a hybrid‑evidence retrieval enhanced RAG framework dedicated to hallucination suppression for AIGC text generation.

LW‑Diffusion: An Optimized Lightweight AIGC Text‑to‑Image Diffusion Model for Resource‑Constrained Edge Devices
Hu, Zhixin. Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology, University of Malaya, Malaysia.

Abstract: Generative‑AI text‑to‑image diffusion models possess powerful multi‑modal content‑generation capabilities. Nevertheless, vanilla diffusion models contain massive parameter volumes and demand numerous iterative sampling steps; most of them can only be deployed on high‑performance cloud GPU servers, which makes direct local execution infeasible for resource‑constrained edge‑side hardware such as mobile smartphones and embedded IoT devices. This paper proposes LW‑Diffusion, an integrated lightweight‑optimization solution for AIGC text‑to‑image diffusion models targeting memory‑ and compute‑limited edge‑side devices.
